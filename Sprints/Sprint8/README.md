# Sprint 08 – Retrieving Company Eligibility Criteria

## 📌 Objective

The objective of Sprint 8 was to retrieve the company's eligibility criteria before validating a student's placement application.

---

## 📖 Business Requirement

Before checking whether a student is eligible to apply, the application must retrieve the company's eligibility requirements such as minimum CGPA, maximum backlogs, eligible branch, and graduation year.

---

## 🛠️ Tasks Completed

- Retrieved the Company record using SOQL.
- Queried only the fields required for eligibility validation.
- Stored the Company record in an Apex object.
- Prepared the retrieved data for comparison with student details.
