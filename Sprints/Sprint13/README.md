# Sprint 13 – Understanding How Triggers Think

## Objective

The objective of this sprint was to understand the role of Salesforce Triggers in enterprise applications before implementing them.

---

## Topics Covered

- What is a Trigger?
- Trigger Responsibilities
- Service Responsibilities
- Before vs After Events
- Business Timing
- Separation of Responsibilities
- Enterprise Trigger Architecture

---

## Engineering Principles Learned

- A Trigger should observe business events.
- Business logic belongs inside Service classes.
- Trigger timing depends on business requirements.
- One business event can start multiple independent business processes.

---

## Source Code

No Apex Trigger implementation was introduced in this sprint.

The focus of this sprint was understanding enterprise Trigger architecture before writing code.
