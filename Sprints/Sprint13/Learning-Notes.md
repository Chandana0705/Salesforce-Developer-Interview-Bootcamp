# Learning Notes – Sprint 13

## Overview

This sprint focused on understanding the architecture behind Salesforce Triggers.

## Concepts Learned

- Triggers observe business events.
- Service classes perform business logic.
- Before events are used for validation.
- After events are used for notifications and updates.
- Business timing determines Trigger timing.
- One business event can initiate several business processes.

## Key Takeaways

- Keep Triggers small.
- Place business logic inside Service classes.
- Design software based on business events rather than platform events.
- Maintain separation of responsibilities.
