# Reflection – Sprint 12

Sprint 12 helped me understand how individual Salesforce concepts come together to build a complete enterprise application. Instead of focusing on one concept, I learned how SOQL, Apex, DML, and business validations work together to process a student's application from start to finish.

This sprint also reinforced the importance of following the correct sequence in a business transaction. Validating business rules before performing DML ensures data integrity and prevents incorrect information from being stored.

Overall, this sprint strengthened my understanding of enterprise application design and showed me how professional Salesforce developers build complete business workflows.
