# Learning Notes – Sprint 12

## Overview

Sprint 12 focused on integrating all previously implemented functionality into one complete business workflow.

## Concepts Learned

- Understood how SOQL and DML work together.
- Learned the sequence of a business transaction.
- Understood why business validation must occur before DML.
- Learned the importance of service-oriented design.
- Understood how enterprise applications process business requests.

## Key Takeaways

- Every business transaction follows a sequence.
- SOQL retrieves business information.
- Business rules are validated before changing data.
- DML should be the final step.
- Good software is built by combining multiple small responsibilities.
