# Reflection – Sprint 11

Sprint 11 helped me understand how Salesforce updates existing records using Apex. Instead of creating a new Application record, I learned how to retrieve an existing record, modify its status, and save the changes using the `update` DML statement.

This sprint also reinforced the importance of exception handling during database operations. I now understand that updating records is an essential part of many business processes, such as recruitment workflows, approval processes, and customer support systems.

Overall, Sprint 11 strengthened my understanding of record modification and DML operations in Salesforce.
