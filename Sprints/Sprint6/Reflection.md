# Reflection – Sprint 06

Sprint 6 helped me understand the importance of writing clean and maintainable Apex code. Instead of placing all the business logic inside a single method, I learned how to divide it into smaller helper methods with specific responsibilities.

This sprint taught me that well-structured code is easier to read, debug, test, and enhance in the future. Refactoring also helped me understand the concept of code reusability and the Single Responsibility Principle.

Overall, Sprint 6 improved my coding practices and gave me a better understanding of how professional Salesforce developers organize Apex code in enterprise applications.
