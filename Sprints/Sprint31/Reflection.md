# Reflection – Sprint 31

Sprint 31 helped me understand how individual Lightning Web Components can work together to form a complete Salesforce application.

During this sprint, I tested the complete Student Placement Portal workflow starting from the Student Profile. After updating the profile, the Eligible Jobs component refreshed and displayed the current eligibility information.

I then tested the JobCard functionality by viewing job details and submitting an application. A new application was successfully created through the Student Portal and was displayed in My Applications.

I also tested duplicate application handling. When I attempted to apply for a job that I had already applied for, the system displayed an appropriate message and prevented a duplicate application.

This sprint helped me understand the importance of component communication, data refresh, server-side business validation, reusable components, and end-to-end testing.

Overall, Sprint 31 gave me a better understanding of how the different Salesforce components work together as one complete application rather than as separate features.
