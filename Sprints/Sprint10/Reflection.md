# Reflection – Sprint 10

Sprint 10 helped me understand how Salesforce stores business data using DML operations. I learned that creating a record is the final step in a business transaction and should only happen after all validations have passed.

I also gained practical experience handling common errors such as missing required fields and data type mismatches. Using exception handling improved my understanding of how to build reliable Salesforce applications.

Overall, this sprint strengthened my knowledge of DML, transaction flow, and responsible data handling in Apex.
