# Reflection – Sprint 04

Sprint 4 helped me understand how business rules are implemented in Salesforce using Apex. I learned how to retrieve data from multiple objects and compare field values to determine whether a student is eligible to apply for a company.

One of the most important lessons from this sprint was understanding that validations should always be performed before saving records. I also learned how the `return` statement improves efficiency by stopping execution when a validation fails.

This sprint strengthened my understanding of conditional logic, SOQL, and business rule implementation, which are essential skills for Salesforce development.
